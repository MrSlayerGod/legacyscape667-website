Using the explorer

Search using a name, a single ID (4151), comma-separated IDs (4310,14887) or a range (4000-4020). You can bookmark records or copy links to share on your wiki.

Keyboard: / focuses search, Esc closes dialogs, and ←/→ move between pages when you are not typing.

The uploaded JSON includes cache definitions but no rendered model images or item sprites. Model IDs are available in the inspector.
